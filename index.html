SYMBIOSIS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SYMBIOSIS ("we," "our," or "the Game") collects, uses, and handles information when you play the Game.

1. Information We Collect and Use

  • Local Gameplay & Progress Data: Game settings, progress, unlocks, scores, and boss completion records are stored locally on your device to support core gameplay functionality.
  • Leaderboard Data (CheddaBoards): When using online leaderboard features, gameplay metrics (such as score, bosses defeated, elapsed time, and your chosen player nickname) are transmitted to and processed by CheddaBoards to display rankings and track personal bests.
  • Online Co-op / Multiplayer Data: When using online multiplayer features, network connection metadata and real-time session data (such as room codes, player inputs, and gameplay synchronization states) are exchanged between connected peers to enable co-op play.

2. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

The Game interacts with third-party services and network infrastructure to provide online features:

  • CheddaBoards: Used for global solo and co-op leaderboard rankings, score submissions, and nickname management.
  • WebRTC / STUN / Signaling Trackers: Used to establish peer-to-peer connections and synchronize online co-op sessions.

Data processed by third-party services is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

3. Data Retention & User Choices

  • Local save data and settings remain on your device until cleared through your device's application settings or uninstallation.
  • You can choose whether to participate in online co-op or set a public leaderboard nickname.

4. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed toward children under the age required by applicable law to consent to data processing without parental consent. If you believe information has been submitted inappropriately, please contact us so we can assist.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

本隱私權政策說明 SYMBIOSIS（以下簡稱「本遊戲」）在您遊玩過程中如何蒐集、使用及處理相關資訊。

1. 我們蒐集與使用的資訊

  • 本機遊戲進度與設定資料：遊戲設定、關卡進度、解鎖項目、分數與 Boss 擊破紀錄會儲存在您的裝置本機，以提供完整遊戲功能。
  • 排行榜資料（CheddaBoards）：當您使用線上排行榜功能時，遊戲成績（包含分數、擊破 Boss 數量、通關時間及您自行設定的玩家暱稱）會傳送至 CheddaBoards 服務進行處理，以顯示全球排名與個人最佳紀錄。
  • 線上雙人連線資料：當您使用線上連線共鬥功能時，連線中繼資訊與即時遊戲同步資料（如房間代碼、操作輸入與角色狀態同步封包）會在連線裝置之間傳輸，以維持多人遊玩體驗。

2. 第三方服務與網路基礎設施

為提供線上排行榜與連線功能，本遊戲會與以下第三方服務或網路節點互動：

  • CheddaBoards：用於單人與合作模式全球排行榜、成績上傳及暱稱管理。
  • WebRTC / STUN / 信令伺服器（Trackers）：用於建立點對點（P2P）連線及同步多人遊戲狀態。

3. 資料保存與您的選擇

  • 本機存檔與設定將保留在您的裝置內，直到您透過裝置應用程式設定清除資料或移除本遊戲為止。
  • 您可以自由決定是否使用線上連線功能或設定公開排行榜暱稱。

4. 兒童隱私保護

本遊戲並非針對未達法定同意年齡之兒童設計。若您認為有兒童在未經監護人同意下提供了相關資訊，請透過下方聯絡方式與我們聯繫。
